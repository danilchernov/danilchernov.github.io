Keepnah privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026

Русская версия

Keepnah is an app for clearing unwanted photos and videos out of your gallery. This policy explains which data leaves your device when you use Keepnah, who receives it and how you can control it. It is the same for every app store Keepnah is published in.

In short

  • Your photos and videos are never sent anywhere. The app views, groups and deletes them on the device only.
  • The app itself sends no data to our servers; we have none.
  • The app includes third-party components from Yandex: an advertising SDK (Yandex Mobile Ads), the AppMetrica analytics service and Varioqub, AppMetrica's remote configuration service. They receive the advertising ID, device details and information about how the app is used. They never receive your media files, their names or any other identifiers of your photos and videos.

What stays on your device

This data is kept only in your phone's storage and is not shared with anyone, including us:

  • photos and videos, their thumbnails, dates, sizes and storage locations;
  • your Keep and Nah decisions, the Nah pile, stats and progress;
  • app settings and reminders (these are local notifications).

Gallery access is needed only to show you your pictures and delete the ones you chose. Android performs the deletion after you confirm it.

What Yandex receives

ComponentDataPurpose
Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (ads) The device's advertising ID, other device identifiers, device model and settings, IP address, and data about ad impressions and taps. Showing ads, including personalised ads, counting impressions and preventing fraud. Ads keep Keepnah free.
AppMetrica (analytics) In-app events (listed below), device identifiers including the advertising ID, device model, Android and app version, language, IP address, and crash reports. Usage and crash statistics, so we can fix bugs and tune limits and ad frequency without shipping an update.
Varioqub (remote configuration, part of AppMetrica) AppMetrica's device identifiers, app and Android version, IP address. Fetching the app's current settings, such as limits and ad frequency, without shipping an update. Keepnah sends no events through it.

The events Keepnah sends to AppMetrica:

  • onboarding completed; gallery access granted in full, granted in part or denied;
  • a decision on a card (Keep or Nah) and the kind of Collection, such as Screenshots; an undo;
  • a Clear-out done: how many files were deleted and how much space was freed;
  • a Collection unlocked by watching an ad;
  • an ad shown, watched to the end or failed to load, and where in the app;
  • a rating prompt shown.

Events carry counts and outcomes only. No photos or videos, and none of their names, paths, dates, geotags or other identifiers, are ever included. The app does not turn on AppMetrica's location collection.

Yandex processes this data as an independent operator under its own terms:

  • Yandex Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads SDK Terms of Use
  • AppMetrica SDK Terms of Use

Data is sent over encrypted connections. We do not sell data or share it with anyone other than the components listed above.

How to limit tracking

  • Android with Google services: Settings → Google → Ads. There you can delete or reset the advertising ID. Once it is deleted, apps no longer receive it and ads become non-personalised.
  • Other Android devices (for example Huawei, Honor, Xiaomi): look for Privacy → Ads in Settings and turn off personalised ads or reset the identifier. The exact menu names depend on the manufacturer.
  • iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Without your permission the app does not receive the advertising identifier.
  • To stop all data transfer, uninstall the app.

Retention and deletion

  • Data on the device is kept while the app is installed. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes it. Your photos and videos are not affected, apart from those you already deleted in Keepnah.
  • Yandex keeps the data it receives for the periods stated in its privacy policy. Resetting the advertising ID breaks the link between new data and old.
  • We cannot tell who you are from the data we have. To withdraw consent or ask for data tied to your device in AppMetrica to be deleted, write to the address under Operator. We will reply and pass the request on to Yandex as far as is technically possible. You can also contact Yandex directly.

Children

Keepnah is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect children's data.
